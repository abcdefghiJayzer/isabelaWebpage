About Isabela

As a daily commuter, I’ve experienced the struggles of unreliable and overcrowded transit. Public transportation has long been a challenge in our country, affecting millions. I advocate for change—not just for myself, but for everyone who deserves a safer, more reliable, and commuter-friendly system.

What is SakayBayan?

SakayBayan advocates for better public transit in the Philippines by informing commuters, highlighting solutions, and empowering action to push for a safer, more efficient, and accessible system.

Our Mission

We strive to make public transportation in the Philippines safe, efficient, and accessible. By informing commuters, sharing solutions, and driving action, we push for a transit system that puts people first.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every Filipino commute with ease, safety, and dignity. A reliable transit system will improve daily life and support national progress.
